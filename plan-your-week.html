Plan your week with Outlook

Canvas knows your assignments. Outlook knows practice, games, rehearsals and work. Put them in one calendar and you can see where your homework time actually is.

1. Add your Canvas assignments to Outlook

You do this once. After that, new assignments show up in Outlook on their own.

  1. In Canvas, click Calendar in the menu on the left.
  2. On the right side of the calendar, under the small month calendar, click Calendar Feed. Copy the link that appears.
  3. Open Outlook: outlook.office.com in Edge, or Outlook on your laptop. Sign in with your RJ account if it asks.
  4. Go to the Calendar. In the left column, click Add calendar, then Subscribe from web.
  5. Paste the link. Name the calendar Canvas, pick a color, and click Import.

Keep the Calendar Feed link to yourself. Anyone who has it can see your assignment list. If you shared it by accident, tell Ed Tech.

Good to know:

  • Outlook checks Canvas for changes a few times a day. It can take up to a day. For what's due right now, Canvas and RJ Tasks for Students are always up to date.
  • Personal to-dos you add in Canvas or RJ Tasks for Students don't come over to Outlook. Put those straight in Outlook if you want them there.
  • You can't edit Canvas items in Outlook. Change them in Canvas.
  • Using classic Outlook on your laptop? The same option is under Add Calendar > From Internet. Doing it at outlook.office.com is easiest.

2. Add your practices, games and rehearsals

Do this once at the start of each season, or when your schedule changes.

  1. In Outlook's calendar, click New event.
  2. Give it a clear title, like Soccer practice or Play rehearsal. Set the start and end time, and include travel time if you have it.
  3. Click Repeat (or Make recurring), choose Weekly, pick the days, and set the end date to the last day of the season.
  4. Click Save. Add games, meets and performances one at a time as you get the schedule.

If your coach or director shares a calendar link for the team schedule, add it the same way as the Canvas feed (Add calendar > Subscribe from web). Then changes to the schedule show up on their own.

Also worth adding: a job, music lessons, service hours, family commitments, and the bus or carpool time you can't use for homework.

3. Turn on reminders on your phone

  1. Install the Microsoft Outlook app from the App Store or Google Play, and sign in with your RJ account.
  2. Open the calendar in the app. Your activities and your Canvas calendar should be there. If Canvas isn't, give it a little while to sync.
  3. Allow notifications for Outlook when your phone asks, or turn them on in your phone's settings.
  4. When you create an event, set a reminder, for example 30 minutes before practice so you leave on time.

4. The 5-minute Sunday plan

Once a week, before the week starts:

  1. Open Outlook and switch to the Week view.
  2. Look at what's due (the Canvas color) and what's already taken (practice, games, work).
  3. Find the open time and block it: make an event like Study: Bio lab report in a free slot before the day it's due.
  4. Busy night with a game and a test the next day? Move studying earlier in the week now, not that night.
  5. In Canvas, check RJ Tasks for Students: Week at a glance shows heavy days, and Catch Up shows anything you've missed.

If something doesn't work

  • No "Subscribe from web" option, or an error when you import: make sure you're at outlook.office.com signed in with your RJ account. If it still fails, tell Ed Tech with the RJ Tasks for Students form or a Help Desk ticket.
  • An assignment is missing from Outlook: if it has no due date in Canvas, it can't appear on a calendar. If it was just posted, wait for Outlook to refresh. Check Canvas for the latest.
  • You want to remove the Canvas calendar: in Outlook, right-click the Canvas calendar in the left column and choose Remove.
