RJ Tasks for Students Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. RJ Tasks for Students is a Microsoft Edge extension made by Regis Jesuit High School Ed Tech for Regis Jesuit students and staff.

The short version

RJ Tasks for Students does not collect, store, sell or share your personal information. Everything it shows you stays in your browser.

What RJ Tasks for Students reads

When you are signed in to Regis Jesuit's Canvas site (rjhs.instructure.com), RJ Tasks for Students uses your existing Canvas sign-in to read your own course list, course colors, planner items, missing assignments and the grades you can already see in Canvas. It uses this only to show you what's due, what's missing and what to work on next, on that same page.

What RJ Tasks for Students changes

Only what you click: marking an item done, adding or removing a personal to-do in your Canvas planner, or marking an announcement as read. These are the same changes Canvas's own planner makes.

What RJ Tasks for Students stores

Your RJ Tasks for Students settings (for example, which sections to show) are saved in your browser's extension storage. If you sign in to Edge, Edge may sync those settings between your own devices. No assignments, grades or Canvas data are stored by RJ Tasks for Students.

What RJ Tasks for Students sends

Nothing leaves your browser except requests to rjhs.instructure.com, the Canvas site you are already using. RJ Tasks for Students has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party services, and it runs no code downloaded from the internet. It does nothing on any other website.

Browser permissions

RJ Tasks for Students asks for one permission, "storage," to save your settings. It runs only on pages at rjhs.instructure.com.

Students

RJ Tasks for Students is provided by Regis Jesuit for its students as part of the school's Canvas learning environment. It shows students only their own information, which they can already see in Canvas.

Bug reports and feature requests

If you use the RJ Tasks for Students bug report and feature request form, your response goes to Regis Jesuit Ed Tech through Microsoft Forms, which requires an RJ sign-in and records your name so Ed Tech can follow up. That form is separate from the extension.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: submit a Regis Jesuit Help Desk ticket or use the form above.
